📋 Nepal Officer Level English — Syllabus & Exam Pattern (click to expand)

PSC Section Officer (English Language)

  • Section A: Essay writing on current national/social issues (about 800–1000 words at officer level).
  • Section B: Translation — English ⇄ Nepali, without losing sense or spirit.
  • Section C: Reading comprehension passage with questions and answers.
  • Section D: Summarisation, speech / Note Verbale / diplomatic note writing.

Nepal Army Officer Cadet & APF Inspector

  • Part One (objective): articles, transformation, question tags, prepositions, direct/indirect speech, active/passive voice, causative verbs, conditionals, tenses, subject–verb agreement, conjunctions.
  • Part Two (subjective): translation both ways, comprehension (~300 words, 5 questions), essay / personal letter / congratulation or condolence note (250–300 words).
  • Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms and word usage.
